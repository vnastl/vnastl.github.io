Hi!

I’m a PhD student at the [Max Planck ETH Center for Learning Systems](https://learning-systems.org/), where I’m fortunate to be advised by [Moritz Hardt](https://mrtz.org/), [Nicolai Meinshausen](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~nicolai/) and [Peter Bühlmann](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~buhlmann/).
I’m part of the Social Foundations of Computation Department at MPI for Intelligent Systems (MPI-IS) in Tübingen, Germany, as well as the Seminar of Statistics in the Department of Mathematics at ETH Zürich. My work is supported by a [Google Ph.D. Fellowship](https://blog.google/outreach-initiatives/google-org/phd-fellowship-program-2025/) in the category “Human-Computer Interaction”.

I expect to graduate in summer 2027 and am looking for postdoc opportunities at the intersection of artificial intelligence and social science.

My research focuses on artificial intelligence for social data. I am interested in unlocking its value to address policy questions and examining how best to engage with it.
